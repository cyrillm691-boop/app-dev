# **The How's of Us (2018)**

The How's of Us
is a romantic drama film directed by **Cathy Garcia-Molina**, starring Kathryn Bernardo and Daniel Padilla. It follows the journey of a couple navigating the complexities of love, career, and life choices.
Plot Summary

The story revolves around George (Kathryn Bernardo) and Primo (Daniel Padilla), who have been together for years but face the struggles of growing up and dealing with the pressures of their future. As they try to balance their personal ambitions and relationship, they question whether their love can survive the challenges of adulthood.

---

Main Character
- George – *Kathryn Bernardo
  A passionate young woman who dreams of becoming a successful musician but faces obstacles in her relationship.
  
- Primo – *Daniel Padilla*  
  A loving partner who wants to support George but also has his own dreams and ambitions.
  
- Supporting Characters  
  - George's Family – Provides emotional support but also adds pressure on the couple.
  - Primo's Family – Their expectations and issues with George create tension in the relationship.
